## Info

The Quantum ProDrive ELS has a rubber stop bumper inside that turns into sticky goo over time, causing the read/write head to stick to it, which prevents the drive from starting up.

The issue is well described here:  
<https://www.downtowndougbrown.com/2025/03/the-gooey-rubber-thats-slowly-ruining-old-hard-drives/>

I created this 3D printable part that can be used as a spacer, so the read/write head stops on this part instead of the sticky rubber.

## Installation

1. Open the drive and move the lid to the side a bit (preferably in an environment where there isn’t too much dust in the air).  
2. Now the scary part: Power on the drive (SCSI connection is not required) and unstick the head by pushing the back of the read/write head. Push it where the spacer will be installed and in the same direction as the spacer points to the back of the read/write head. This might require a little force.
3. Now Install the spacer by securing it with the existing screw on the coil magnet.  
4. Make sure the spacer prevents the head from sticking to the rubber bumper by turning the drive off and on and see if it now spins up correctly.
5. Close the drive.

## Issues
This is a hacky solution and I don’t have any long-term experience with it. But at the time of writing, my drive is used once a week for about an hour, and I’ve had no issues so far. If the data on your drive is important, you should definitely make a backup.

Because the spacer is made of plastic instead of rubber, the read/write head is quite noisy when the drive powers down. It seems to vibrate back and forth when it hits the plastic on power-down, which appears to be the cause of the noise.
